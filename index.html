2 · Your transmission

Build the frame the briefing asks for, modulate it, and load the IQ here.

Waiting for a file…

What it expects

  • Bell 202 AFSK — 1200 Hz mark, 2200 Hz space, 1200 baud
  • NRZI, HDLC framing, bits least-significant first
  • An AX.25 UI frame: control 0x03, PID 0xF0, CRC-16/X.25 sent little-endian
  • Narrowband FM onto a baseband carrier at zero offset

Tone polarity does not matter — NRZI is differential. Neither does how much flag preamble you send.
